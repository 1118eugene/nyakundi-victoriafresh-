# Owner facts to confirm before publication

The storefront avoids making claims where the project does not provide a
verified current answer. Please confirm these details before publishing
operational promises:

| Fact | Owner confirmation needed |
| --- | --- |
| Delivery areas | Confirm the counties, towns and specific areas served; the checkout currently uses the delivery zones configured in PostgreSQL. |
| Delivery times | Confirm the normal delivery estimates and whether estimates vary by location or order. |
| Delivery fees | Confirm the fee schedule and any minimum order or other delivery conditions for each service area. Checkout displays configured fees. |
| Opening hours | Confirm business, order-support and eatery opening hours, including days closed. |
| Contact details | Confirm the public phone number, email address and WhatsApp account to publish. |
| Refund and return rules | Confirm what customers should do if an order is incorrect or does not meet expectations, and which remedies may be offered. The site currently asks customers to contact the team; it does not promise a refund or replacement. |
| Dine-in | Confirm whether dine-in service is available, its location and any conditions. The current catalogue includes ready-to-eat items, but this does not establish dine-in availability. |
| Cooked-food delivery | Confirm the locations, order process and payment options available for prepared meals. |
| Product handling at handoff | Confirm any item-specific storage instructions to provide at delivery. The current general advice is to chill raw fish promptly, keep it separate from ready-to-eat food, and follow local food-safety guidance. |
| Order changes and cancellations | Confirm when a customer may request a change or cancellation and how the team decides whether it can be accepted. |
| Payment-on-delivery options | Confirm that Cash on Delivery and M-Pesa on delivery are available in each service area and any operational conditions. |

Until confirmed, use neutral copy such as “Delivery fee is shown at checkout”,
“Delivery availability depends on the area configured at checkout”, and
“Contact us to confirm”.
